# Changelog

## 1.2.3

- First release on Thunderstore. No gameplay changes.

## 1.2.2

- Fixed the betting board's "2 to 1" buttons. The one beside 3–36 actually bet on 1–34, so your chips showed up on the wrong row and the bet paid on the wrong numbers. Each button now bets on its own row.

## 1.2.1

- Fixed the wheel spinning a second time, right after it stopped, for players without the mod.

## 1.2.0

- Players without the mod can now play at a modded host's table by betting in chat: `!bet`, `!bets`, `!clear`, `!spin` and `!help`.
- Each spot on the table now shows the total of everyone's chips, instead of a single player's stack.
- Players without the mod can no longer open the table, whose menu is broken in their game and used to trap them in the table camera. They bet by chat instead.
- Roulette messages appear in the chat log without speech bubbles, and private replies (`!help`, `!bets`) only go to you.
- New settings: `ChatBetting`, `ChatBetMaxPerSpin` and `BlockTableWithoutMod`.

## 1.1.0 – 1.1.1

- No changes to the mod itself (installer updates for the GitHub download).

## 1.0.3

- The table now appears inside the lodge arcade, by the VIP fence and the big prize wheel.

## 1.0.2

- Fixed F8 not moving the table.

## 1.0.1

- Fixed a click placing a bet on the last square you hovered when the mouse had already left the board, including clicks on `+`, `−` and clear.

## 1.0.0

- First release: hosts get the roulette table back, with the full betting board, chips and wheel. The host can move it with F8, and gamepad players can use the betting board.
